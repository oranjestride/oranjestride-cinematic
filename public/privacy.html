Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data OranjeStride Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. ("OranjeStride", "we") collects through oranjestride.com, why, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, organisation, email address, enquiry type and message — only what you choose to send us.
  • Usage analytics: we use Plausible Analytics, which is cookieless and does not collect personal data or track you across sites. It gives us aggregate figures such as page views and referring sites.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting provider and font provider receive your IP address and browser details when your browser requests the page.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell personal data.

Why we use it

We use enquiry details only to respond to you and, if you go ahead, to arrange and deliver the training or consulting you asked about. We do not add you to marketing lists without your consent.

Who processes it for us

  • Formspree — receives enquiry-form submissions and forwards them to our team.
  • Vercel — hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts — serves the site's typefaces.
  • Plausible Analytics — privacy-first, aggregate site statistics.

These providers may process data outside India, under their own security and privacy commitments.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to maintain any resulting business relationship, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase the personal data we hold about you, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance. Email contactus@oranjestride.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
